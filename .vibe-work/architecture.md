### Navigation Groups (in AppShell)

- **Core**: Focus (/daily), Read (/library), Timer (/timer), Settings (/settings)
- **Library**: Doctrine, Supplemental, Sermons, Bookmarks
- **Settings**: Reading & Display, Focus & Practice, Timer Defaults, About & Legal
